## STAGE 2 — THE DESIGN (Design Constitution — climax-first order is MANDATORY)

### 1. Climax Moment (Constitution, Climax Rule)
One sentence. Visceral, emotional, sensory, consequential, repeatable. If it can't be said in one sentence, stop here.

### 2. World-Changing Lesson
One sentence. The encounter teaches exactly ONE new thing about the world, permanently.

### 3. Core Question
The single survival question this encounter asks ("Should I ___?"). **Checked against the other three docs — no two encounters ask the same question (Pillar 3).**

### 4. The Threat & Threat Ambiguity
What is ACTUALLY dangerous (environment / mechanism / absence / assumption / schedule / exit / entity)? What does the player initially THINK is dangerous? The reveal must feel inevitable in hindsight, never arbitrary.

### 5. Discovery Design (Pillar 1 — discovery precedes understanding; never explain)
How the player finds out: sound? observation? consequence? contradiction? absence? Choreography: evidence before entity → partial glimpses → full sight only at maximum dramatic cost (horror-craft).

### 6. The Five Acts (each act progressively shorter; brevity is power)
- **Act I — Arrival:**
- **Act II — Discovery:**
- **Act III — Escalation:**
- **Act IV — Climax:** (the Stage 2 §1 moment)
- **Act V — Resolution:** (quick; clears space for what's next)

### 7. Choice Points (Pillar 3)
2–3 genuinely valid approaches, each viable but risky. The "what if?" the player exits with.

### 8. Information Map (Pillar 4 — Trust)
Every piece of survival information and WHERE it lives (hidden / subtle / in sound / in absence / in contradiction). Losing because info wasn't available = bad design. No invisible tricks.

### 9. Failure Design (Constitution, Failure Philosophy)
What failure teaches · how fast it lands (no doomed slow deaths) · why it never feels unfair · why the player immediately retries.

### 10. Sound Design (Audio law + Eddy 2026-07-06: **NO MUSIC — SFX, ambience, silence only**)
Every sound answers a question (what happened? where? dangerous? did I succeed?). Where silence is deployed as the loudest tool. Directional cues honest in geometry.

### 11. The Space (encounter-design: "the room is an extension of the monster itself")
Room identity, readable silhouettes, what the environment communicates before anything speaks. Wrongness felt before it's noticed (The Threshold: impossible, indifferent).

### 12. Mobile Sanity Notes (ship-real — ~70% of Roblox is mobile)
Readability on a small bright screen · phone-speaker audio cues · touch interaction · low-end performance implications.

### 13. The Cut List (Climax Rule step 7)
What was designed and then REMOVED because it didn't serve the climax. (Empty cut list = the cut pass didn't happen.)
