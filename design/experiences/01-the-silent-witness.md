**Archetype:** The Silent Witness — paranoia / violation (being watched, recorded, studied)
**Designer:** Eddy + Claude · **Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Status:** **Whiteboard — GATE A PENDING**
**Target playtest utterance (pass criterion):** *"it's been watching me this whole time?"* — unprompted, at least once per playtest.

## STAGE 1 — THE WHITEBOARD

**Inspiration** — The Closing Shift's retroactive photo-wall reveal, ECHO's replay-of-your-own-conduct, Anatomy's house-that-gatekeeps, P.T.'s corrupted footstep echo — extracted, not copied: their stalkers are obsessed with you; The Threshold's watcher is **indifferent**. You are not loved or hated. You are *specimen data*. (Research: [craft-silent-witness.md](../research/craft-silent-witness.md) findings 1, 8, 9, 10.)

**Emotion** — **Violation.** Arc: unease (am I alone?) → confirmation (I am not) → violation (I never was, and it wrote everything down).

**Hook** — Every room the player enters produces one dry mechanical *click* — small, sourceless, consistent. Nothing follows. By the third room the player has a theory; by the fifth, a ritual: enter, freeze, listen. The first room that **doesn't** click is scarier than any that did. (Sound kit: craft study finding 13.)

**Memorable Moment** — The player finds the annex behind the walls: a filing room. Hundreds of dossiers. One is open on the desk — **theirs**. Their route through the previous rooms marked on a floor plan. Tally marks matching their actual door entries. Their pauses, timed. And the last page: a sketch of them in their current idle pose — **mid-stroke, unfinished**. Whatever draws was here a moment ago. Or still is. Every mark is forensically true because the server logged the real session — when a streamer checks the tallies against their VOD, they match. That is the "THIS AIN'T REAL" beat: the game proves it was watching.

**Experience in one sentence** — *You realize the place has been taking notes on you — and you find the notebook.*

**Replayability** — The dossier is generated from the live session log, so it is **never the same document twice**: crawl the whole run and the notes say so; sprint and they say that instead. Players replay to test what it records ("does it count how long I stared at the painting?") — theory-testing is the replay engine, and the community comparison of dossiers is the conversation engine.

**Multiplayer** — *What changes when a second human is present?* The dossiers cross-reference: "Subject B waits for Subject A before every door." The violation doubles — it studied your **friendship**. (Prototype: single-player; field carried per audit F13.)

**Runtime Test** — Deserves to exist because no Roblox horror game has ever shown the player verifiable evidence of their own session as the scare. Observation horror is the platform's rising paradigm (front-page teardown, 2026-07) — this is its endgame: the player *is* the content.

### The Five Questions
1. **What inspired this?** Watched-horror canon (P.T., Closing Shift, Anatomy, ECHO) transformed through indifference — the watcher takes notes the way a researcher does, not the way a stalker does.
2. **What emotion?** Paranoia resolving into violation.
3. **What question is the player answering?** **"Do I let it see me?"** — moving fast feeds it motion; standing still feeds it detail; every behavior is authored testimony. (No other encounter asks this.)
4. **What is the unforgettable moment?** Opening your own unfinished dossier.
5. **Why will players remember this?** Because the evidence was *really theirs* — the game replays your own conduct back at you, and that violation survives after quitting.

### Runtime Test checks
Curiosity: the click ritual begs investigation ✓ · Fun: detective-work against the room itself ✓ · Memory: your own dossier ✓ · Conversation: "check the tallies against my VOD" ✓ · Originality: no stalker, no entity, no photos of a victim — clinical specimen files ✓ · Purpose: proves the studio thesis "the room is an extension of the monster" with zero monsters ✓

### Mechanical spine (for Gate A honesty — full design is Stage 2)
Baseline-then-deviation rooms (P.T.-loop literacy; Exit 8 categorical-change fairness: silhouette-scale changes only, always a second corroborating trace) · unobserved-reorientation tells (the chair now faces the door you came in through — never seen moving, always seen *having* moved) · footstep echo returning one half-beat late (stops late when you stop) · server session log powering the dossier (ECHO device: playback of a real log, no AI) · death/failure text as the place's clinical observation log ("Subject held the handle 3.1 seconds. Subject did not look up."). All server-authoritative; the dossier references **only this session's in-game behavior** — never account data, never chat.
