## Doc 01 — lens: constitution

Cut the late footstep echo from 01 entirely and reserve corrupted-body-audio exclusively for 03's tape climax; a player who runs 01 first must not arrive at 03's unforgettable moment already taught what it means. **Verdict:** This is the strongest kind of doc to audit — research-genuinely-used (every cited finding in craft-silent-witness.md checks out), climax truly one sentence, lesson truly one thing, discovery organic, no jump scares, no RNG, no reflex tests, real replay engine, server-authoritative by construction — and it still fails four gate boxes when read literally: "all info available" (the only warning in the encounter is silence masked by its own taught silence rule, with no visual sibling for the muted mobile majority), "consistent / no invisible tricks" (the 1:1 click rule is broken once with no authored meaning, and the dossier itself will prove the tell lied), "progressively shorter" (Acts II and III are both ~3 min yet the box is ticked), and "respects time" on the failure side (re-file costs ~10 authored minutes to reach the retry payoff). ## Doc 01 — lens: trust-breaker

- **[critical / confirmed]** _TRUST (losing to unavailable info = design failure) + Audio law (every load-bearing audio cue needs a visual sibling) + Failure (punishment must never land before its warning)_
  - Evidence: §8: "warning pause = the scratch stops dead for two seconds when the player raises a hand to the wall or the dossier — the place's only 'no'"; §9: "warned once by the two-second dead-stop." §10's silent-sibling list ("reoriented chair, chalk, tallies, dust") covers the tells but NOT the warning. The game's only warning is a purely auditory absence-cue.
  - Proposed fix: Give the warning a categorical visual sibling that fires simultaneously (e.g., every light in the room holds a visible dim, or the nearest wall shows the pencil line stop mid-stroke and hang there). The doc's own cited research (craft-silent-witness.md, Mobile notes) mandates this: 'every load-bearing audio cue needs a silent sibling.' Failure scenario as written: a muted mobile player (studio's own data: ~70% mobile, many muted) taps the dossier to tear it, receives literally zero warning through any channel they possess, and is re-filed to room one — losing 10 minutes of a 10–15 minute session to information that never existed for them. This is the exact Exit 8 trust break the research file names ('a costly punishment carrying no lesson').
- **[critical / confirmed]** _TRUST (world never lies about a learned rule) + Anti-pattern (hidden mid-crisis rule change)_
  - Evidence: Act III / §7 / §8 teach that the scratch "pauses when they pause" ("scratch-pause = it reacts to your stillness"). The warning is the same physical signal — the scratch going silent — and it fires exactly when the player has necessarily stopped moving to interact with the wall or dossier. The benign mirror-pause fully masks the warning at the only moment the warning matters.
  - Proposed fix: The warning signal is semantically colliding with an already-taught rule. Failure scenario: player walks to the wall, stops (scratch pauses — learned meaning: 'it pauses with me'), raises hand (scratch 'stops dead' — indistinguishable from the pause already in progress), strikes, gets re-filed, and correctly reports 'it never warned me; it always goes quiet when I stand still.' Secondary collision: reading the dossier — the sanctioned exit-relevant action — also requires raising a hand to it, so either the warning fires on readers (who then read, suffer nothing, and learn the 'no' is hollow — cry-wolf corrupting the rule before the wall) or the game psychically discriminates intent, which is unimplementable. Fix: give the 'no' a distinct signature that cannot be produced by player stillness (e.g., a single hard knock from inside the wall, or all entry-clicks sounding at once), and bind the warning to the destructive verb only — a two-step hold-to-tear/strike interaction whose wind-up window IS the warning and can be aborted.
- **[major / confirmed]** _TRUST (world never lies about a learned rule) — internal mechanic contradiction_
  - Evidence: §8: "Click = entry logged (1:1, testable)" and Stage 1: "Every mark is forensically true... when a streamer checks the tallies against their VOD, they match" versus §10: "the one room that doesn't click (Act III)." The doc never defines what the silent room means for the record.
  - Proposed fix: As taught, no-click implies not-logged. If that room's entry then appears in the dossier's route plan and tallies, the world lied about its one most rigorously taught rule — and the flagship streamer-VOD verification is exactly where the community will catch it (VOD shows N room entries, N-1 clicks, N tallies). If instead the entry is absent from the dossier, the 'forensically complete' record is provably incomplete. Define a truthful resolution and write it into §8: e.g., the silent room's page in the dossier is already fully inked before the player arrives — it didn't click because it had finished studying them there — so the silence corroborates the record instead of contradicting it (and is arguably scarier). Also note the silent-room beat is null for muted players; its Act III placement as 'the loudest tool' needs a visual sibling of absence (e.g., that room's baseline object already reoriented on FIRST entry).
- **[major / confirmed]** _TRUST (all survival/world info exists in-world; doc's own hard ban: "no psychic reveals... nothing untraceable") + doc's own fairness citation (escalations fair only if 'every new capability traces to something the player actually did while observed')_
  - Evidence: §6 Act III: "their habitual route pre-opened; their favored hiding spot chalk-marked" — in an entity-less, forward-moving, first-run encounter (~7 min before Act III, nothing to hide from per Act I 'nothing threatens'), many players will have no hiding behavior and no repeated route in the session log at all.
  - Proposed fix: Failure scenario: player never crouches, never enters a closet, walks each corridor once; Act III chalk-marks a 'favored hiding spot' anyway; player checks their own memory/VOD — 'I never went there' — and the game's central promise (every mark is really yours) is publicly false; this is adjacent to Alien: Isolation's certified trust-killer of psychic knowledge of hiding spots, named in the doc's own research. Gate B defect #2 fixed the dossier under degenerate input but not the Act III escalations. Fix: make every escalation conditional on its log entry existing (chalk mark only if a lingering/occlusion event was logged; pre-opened doors only if a door was entered ≥2 times — the Act I loop guarantees route data, so route escalation is safe; hiding-spot escalation needs a sparse-log fallback such as the pause-timer marks). State the conditionality rule explicitly in §8.
- **[major / confirmed]** _Internal contradiction (stated mechanic contradicts another stated mechanic) — risks the CHOICE pillar (fake choice) if resolved the wrong way in implementation_
  - Evidence: §7 choice 3: "read it fully (deepest violation; the exit condition)" directly contradicts the adjacent option "leave without reading (exit permitted)" and Gate B defect #1's fix "reading is optional, finding is not."
  - Proposed fix: Two mutually exclusive exit rules are in the doc simultaneously. If a builder implements read-gated exit, 'leave without reading' becomes a fake choice (anti-pattern) and the doc's own defect-#1 fix is undone. Correct §7 to match defect #1: entering the annex is the exit condition; reading is optional. One-line edit, but it gates implementation.
- **[major / plausible]** _Failure (never unfair, lands fast, invites retry) + Mobile law (10–15 min sessions; touch interaction) — warning-before-action is mechanically unguaranteed_
  - Evidence: §8's warning trigger is "raises a hand" — an intent pre-phase that does not exist in Roblox touch input, where tap = interact; §9's punishment is "returned to the encounter's first room, rooms re-baselined," i.e., a full Acts I–III replay (~10 min) at minute ~10 of a mobile session. "Warned once" scope (global vs per-object, wall-in-Act-III vs dossier-in-Act-IV) is unspecified.
  - Proposed fix: Failure scenario: mobile player at the dossier fat-taps the destructive prompt (read and tear live on the same object), or taps twice out of habit — the 'raise hand' phase never occurred, so the once-only warning was consumed invisibly or skipped — and the punishment costs the entire remaining session, converting the doc's best moment into the churn point. Fix: (a) specify the destructive interaction as a distinct, deliberately slow verb (hold-to-tear with a visible abortable wind-up = the warning window, per finding 2); (b) specify 'warned once PER target, warning re-armed at the dossier'; (c) reconsider re-file depth — resume at the Act III threshold with the annex remembered, since the dossier-contains-the-attempt beat (the actual lesson) survives either way and the doc's research explicitly flags progress-to-zero resets as a trust break.
- **[minor / plausible]** _Audio law (no stereo-only tells; mono phone speakers) — the fail condition requires localization the majority device cannot perform_
  - Evidence: §9: failure includes "striking the scratch-wall" — but the scratch is mobile ("keeps pace with them") and identifying WHICH wall it is behind is a stereo/spatial-audio task; §12 bans nothing here but studio law bans stereo-only tells, and the doc's replay pitch ("theory-testing is the replay engine") actively invites players to test walls.
  - Proposed fix: Failure scenario: mono-speaker player hears the scratch 'somewhere,' taps a wall to theory-test (behavior the design celebrates), the scratch happens to be behind that wall — provocation registered against a surface the player could not have distinguished from any other wall. Fix: the forbidden surface must be visually self-identifying at silhouette scale (e.g., a faint fresh graphite seam or dust vibration on the active scratch-wall — which doubles as the scratch's missing visual sibling), and/or only a deliberate labeled strike verb counts as provocation, never generic wall interaction.

**Verdict:** The doc's core trust artifact is genuinely strong — the dossier as a forensically true server-side session log is the rare horror premise where the TRUST pillar is the scare rather than a constraint on it, and the room-coarse observation rule, second-corroborating-trace contract, and cut list show real discipline. But the design breaks precisely at its failure system: the single weakest point is that the place's only "no" — the two-second scratch dead-stop — is an audio-only absence cue with no visual sibling that is also semantically identical to an already-taught benign signal (the scratch pauses whenever the player pauses, and the player must pause to interact). On the studio's own majority platform (muted mobile), the game's only punishment can land completely unwarned, and even for players with sound the warning is masked or cried-wolf by the read interaction — a punishment before its warning, which the studio's own research file names as the archetype's certified trust-killer pattern. Secondary fractures: the flagship 1:1 click rule collides undefined with the silent-room beat exactly where the streamer-VOD verification will be watching, two Act III escalations can manifest behaviors the session log never contained (a psychic reveal by the doc's own hard ban), and the exit condition is stated two contradictory ways. All are fixable without touching the climax; none invalidate Gate A. The Honest % of 65 is roughly fair for the concept but generous for the failure system, which as written is closer to 40 and should not pass Gate B until findings 1–2 (warning channel and warning semantics) are redesigned and §7/§8 contradictions are corrected on paper.
