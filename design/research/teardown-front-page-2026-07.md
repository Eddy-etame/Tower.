# Roblox horror front page, July 2026 — live chart check and market gap analysis for Project 001

> T5 research dossier - teardown:front-page-live - generated 2026-07-06. Every finding: mechanism -> causal chain -> verdict -> Threshold translation -> where it lands -> source.

## Summary

DOORS is still the cultural king (7.48B visits, 92.9% rating) but runs at only 14-27K CCU versus its 381K 2023 peak, while Pressure has effectively fallen off the board — deleted and reinstated as an 18+ restricted experience in May 2026 amid a developer scandal, now peaking at ~2.6K CCU from a 112K March 2026 high. The CCU crown moved to horror-adjacent hybrids: 99 Nights in the Forest (303K peak, April 2026) and asymmetric Forsaken (~80K average, RIA 2025 winner), both multiplayer and both accused by the community of diluting actual horror. The genuinely rising pure-horror paradigms are observation horror (official The Exit 8, April 2026, 127M+ visits) and solo rules/service horror (Road Side Shawarma), plus a marketing arms race around adaptive AI entities (The Intruder, The Maze) — all three are single-player-compatible and all three sell fear as information processing, not reflexes. The solo psychological canon players still recommend (Jim's Computer, The Mirror, It Lurks) is 2-4 years old and runs at double-digit CCU, meaning there is demand-proof but no modern flagship for exactly what Project 001 is building. The single biggest lesson: the market has already validated every pillar of The Threshold separately — observation as survival, rules that never lie, unkillable indifferent threats — but no game on the platform combines them in a modern single-player-first package, and the slot beside DOORS is literally vacant.

## Findings

### 1. [ADAPT] The Silent Witness (archetype 1), Act III Escalation; server-side entity-memory system

- **Mechanism:** The Intruder / The Maze: adaptive entity memory — the AI tracks noise, light use, and movement patterns, learns the player's favorite hiding spots, and checks those spots FIRST in later encounters; The Maze's Stalker learns directional habits (always turn right → it starts appearing from the right)
- **Causal chain:** entity remembers prior runs → the player's hard-won safe knowledge decays → uncertainty is renewed every attempt and fear shifts from 'it might find me' to 'it KNOWS me' — dread of being studied
- **Threshold translation:** This is The Silent Witness's core theme made mechanical: being watched, recorded, studied. Server-side memory of player habits (hide spot choice, wait duration, route bias) that the entity references — but bound by our trust law: the learning must leave evidence (your old hiding spot found disturbed, a mark where you last stood), so the player can OBSERVE that they've been learned and adapt. Never invisible cheating.
- **Source:** https://noping.com/blog/best-roblox-horror-games + https://www.robloxdesk.com/scariest-roblox-games/

### 2. [ADAPT] The Hidden Presence (archetype 3), Act II Discovery across all encounters; room/anomaly authoring system

- **Mechanism:** The Exit 8 (official Roblox version, April 2026, 127M+ visits): observation IS the verb — an infinite corridor loop with 36-40 catalogued anomalies across 6 categories (posters, doors, floor tiles, lighting, the Walking Man); the ONLY decision is 'is anything wrong?'; judge correctly to advance, miss and you lose progress
- **Causal chain:** the world may or may not be lying this loop → player scans every surface and memorizes the 'true' state of the corridor → paranoid hypervigilance and self-doubt ('was that poster always crooked?') — fear without any chase
- **Threshold translation:** Direct validation that anomaly-reading charts on Roblox. The Threshold's impossible architecture is this mechanic wearing a world instead of a level: rooms that shouldn't connect ARE the anomaly set. Adopt observation-as-progression; REFUSE the full progress reset on a miss (players farm wikis to memorize 36 anomalies — information living outside the game is our definition of failed design) and refuse the binary right/wrong loop — a missed observation should change the encounter, not delete the session.
- **Source:** https://earnaldo.com/blog/the-exit-8-free-robux-guide + https://www.gamespew.com/2025/08/the-exit-8-walkthrough/ + https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/a-new-version-of-the-exit-8-releases-on-roblox-to-celebrate-the-upcoming-films-premier/

### 3. [ADAPT] The Violent Rhythm (archetype 2) Acts I-III; the no-music audio information system

- **Mechanism:** Road Side Shawarma (solo 'service horror,' by @nv1sh, charting 2026): rules delivered diegetically and ONCE — the manager phone-calls you at the start of every shift with new rules and ingredients; anomaly events have specific counter-rituals (lights die → middle table, crouch, close your eyes, don't look up); Night 2's twin repeats an order the whiteboard won't show, so memory itself is a survival stat; endings are determined by call/item/choice compliance
- **Causal chain:** survival information arrives through one fragile audio channel, once → the player attends totally to sound and holds rules in their head → dread of misremembering plus ritual compliance under fear — tension with zero combat
- **Threshold translation:** This is our AUDIO DIRECTIVE proven commercially: sound is information, every sound answers a question. Adopt diegetic single-delivery rule transmission (a voice, a note, an absence) and counter-ritual design — it fits The Violent Rhythm's panic→understanding→control arc perfectly. REFUSE the trust break inside it: bad endings locked by a missed early call with no in-world recovery path. In The Threshold every rule, once taught, is re-discoverable somewhere (echo, residue, contradiction).
- **Source:** https://roadsideshawarma.com/wiki + https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/road-side-shawarma-a-beginner-s-guide + https://www.droidgamers.com/guides/roadside-shawarma-walkthrough/

### 4. [ADAPT] The Violent Rhythm climax; encounter pacing/escalation system; monetization policy

- **Mechanism:** 99 Nights in the Forest (303K CCU peak, April 2026): The Deer is INVULNERABLE — no weapon damages it, flashlight only stuns; the campfire safe zone burns fuel (safety is a depleting resource); cultist raids arrive on a fixed every-4th-night rhythm that escalates; rescuing kidnapped children lets you sleep-skip nights — progression literally shortens your exposure to danger
- **Causal chain:** combat is removed as an option against the true threat → players reallocate effort to preparation, light economy, and pattern-reading → dread acquires an economy (every log burned is safety spent) and a calendar (you can feel night 4 coming)
- **Threshold translation:** Adopt the invulnerable-threat law wholesale — The Threshold's entities were never killable, and 300K concurrents prove mainstream Roblox accepts a monster you can only understand, never beat. Adopt 'progression = reduced exposure': learning the world's rules shortens or defangs later phases of an encounter (understanding as literal armor). Adopt the fixed escalation rhythm as a promise the world keeps. REFUSE the paid-revive/gear economy that generated its P2W backlash — in a trust game, purchased survival is a lie about the rules.
- **Source:** https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roblox/99-nights-in-the-forest-tips/ + https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/The_Deer

### 5. [ADAPT] World-building canon; Act I Arrival priors in all four archetypes

- **Mechanism:** Forsaken (4B visits, ~80K CCU, RIA 2025 Best Survival Experience): platform-folklore casting — every killer is a real Roblox myth (1x1x1x1, John Doe, c00lkidd, Guest 666), so the horror IP arrives pre-installed in the audience's childhood memory
- **Causal chain:** the player already half-believes the legend from years on the platform → recognition fires before the entity even acts → instant dread plus enormous content-creator fuel (every killer reveal is a video)
- **Threshold translation:** REFUSE the literal move — The Threshold is indifferent and cannot wink at Roblox memes without breaking its own reality. ADAPT the principle: cast fears the audience already carries. Build entities and rooms from universal childhood dread-priors (the reflection that moves late, the hallway that's longer coming back, the door that was never there) so recognition does Forsaken's work without borrowing anyone's mythology.
- **Source:** https://forsaken.wiki/Forsaken + https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/ForsakenRoblox

### 6. [REFUSE] The Silent Witness (archetype 1); input/attention-tracking system

- **Mechanism:** Dead Silence + in-development found-footage games: microphone as game state — voice activity detection means talking or screaming in real life alerts Mary Shaw to your position; several 2025-26 projects use monster AI that localizes players by their actual voice
- **Causal chain:** the mic listens → the player polices their own breathing and involuntary reactions → the body becomes an input and fear becomes self-surveillance
- **Threshold translation:** Refuse as a core mechanic: ~70% of our players are on mobile, voice chat is age-gated 13+, kids play muted in public, and a mechanic keyed to hardware some players lack is unfair variance (a trust break by our law). But extract its essence — the game reacting to what the player does involuntarily — through observation instead: WHERE you point the camera, how long your light lingers, what you chose to look at. Your gaze is your voice; The Silent Witness notices what you notice.
- **Source:** https://hone.gg/blog/roblox-horror-games/ + https://devforum.roblox.com/t/found-footage-style-horror-game-in-development/2933726

### 7. [ADAPT] The Hidden Presence (archetype 3), Act III Escalation; player-tool design

- **Mechanism:** Frigid Dusk: sensory substitution with a cost — when visibility drops, players switch to a grainy thermal camera that shows heat signatures but has limited battery and NO depth perception; you trade one sense for another, never getting the full picture
- **Causal chain:** each viewing mode reveals one truth and hides another → the player must choose which blindness to accept moment to moment → sustained uncertainty even while 'seeing' the threat
- **Threshold translation:** Strong fit for The Hidden Presence: any tool that reveals the entity should cost or occlude something else (a light that shows the room but blinds you to the doorway; a sound cue that only resolves while standing still). Fear stays rooted in incomplete information the player CHOSE, which keeps it fair under the trust law.
- **Source:** https://endsights.com/best-horror-roblox-games + https://noping.com/blog/best-roblox-horror-games

### 8. [ADAPT] The Moral Collapse (archetype 4) and Hidden Presence Act IV; diegetic information channels

- **Mechanism:** Jim's Computer (2022, still on every 2026 solo list): unreliable perception through a diegetic interface — plot and survival information arrives via an in-game computer (News, Store, Chat apps); the anonymous messenger warning you may be imaginary; the horror is that the player cannot verify which channel tells the truth; no visible monsters at all
- **Causal chain:** two information channels contradict → the player doubts the world and then themselves → psychological dread with zero entities on screen
- **Threshold translation:** Adapt with a hard constraint: in The Threshold the WORLD never lies — but the player's interpretation can be wrong, and contradiction between channels is itself information (the room says one thing, the sound says another; the discrepancy IS the clue). REFUSE the self-harm ending outright — Pressure's 18+ reclassification shows Roblox's 2026 moderation will delete or age-gate a game over mature content, which would kill us on a 70%-mobile young platform.
- **Source:** https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/JimsComputer + https://rblx-horror.fandom.com/wiki/Jim%E2%80%99s_Computer

## Trust breaks (their haters are our free QA)

- 99 Nights in the Forest: sustained pay-to-win backlash (purchased gear/revives trivialize survival) across YouTube/TikTok/Facebook — players read bought safety as the game lying about its own stakes; plus the RIA 'Best Horror' award backlash ('nightmarish in name only') shows players punish horror-label dilution
- Pressure: the ultimate non-gameplay trust break — deleted without warning, reinstated as 18+ (locking out most of its own audience), beloved character's voice actor publicly quit; community trust in the game evaporated with the developer's reputation, CCU fell from 112K to ~2.6K
- The Exit 8 (Roblox): missing one subtle anomaly wipes all run progress, so players memorize all 36-40 anomalies from wikis and walkthroughs — when the winning information lives outside the game, the in-game observation contract is broken (for us: needing a wiki IS the trust break)
- DOORS: the community litigates whether Backdoor deaths are RNG in videos like 'Proof That the Backdoor is NOT RNG' — when players cannot tell whether a death was earned or random, they stop trusting the rules and take the argument to YouTube; perceived randomness is as damaging as real randomness
- Dead Silence-style mic detection: players on mobile, muted, or under the 13+ voice-chat gate are effectively playing a different (easier or broken) game — mechanics keyed to hardware/permissions some players lack read as unfair variance
- Road Side Shawarma: bad endings can lock in from a missed call or absent item nights earlier with no warning and no recovery path — a memory tax with retroactive punishment, exactly the 'losing because info wasn't available' failure our Constitution bans

## Mobile notes

Seventy percent of the audience plays on small BRIGHT screens with phone speakers, often muted, in short sessions — which is quietly why the 2026 winners look the way they do. What survives: Exit 8-style observation horror (anomalies are room-scale and obvious-once-seen, not pixel hunts — perfect for a 6-inch screen), Road Side Shawarma's short night-shift loops (10-20 minute complete arcs), 99 Nights' learnable rhythms and readable rules, and any horror built on WRONGNESS rather than darkness — a max-brightness phone kills darkness-dependent horror dead, but a hallway that's longer coming back is terrifying at any brightness. What dies: mic/voice mechanics (age-gated 13+, muted in public, no headset), subtle directional audio as the SOLE information channel (phone speakers are tinny and mono-ish — every critical sound cue needs a visual or spatial redundancy), fine visual detail smaller than a thumb, and long uninterruptible encounters. One trap specific to us: on a phone speaker, pure silence is indistinguishable from a muted phone — silence only reads as information when it's framed by sound the player just heard, so our no-music soundscape must teach the player that the game is never actually silent, and then spend absence deliberately.

## The surprise

Pressure — the genre's presumed #2 and DOORS' heir — no longer meaningfully exists as a market force: it was deleted and reinstated as an 18+ restricted experience in May 2026 after a developer scandal (not a gameplay failure), collapsing from a 112K March peak to ~2.6K CCU and vacating the 'dread-forward horror' slot entirely. The compounding surprise: the current horror CCU crown (99 Nights, 303K) is barely a horror game by its own community's admission, and the closest thing to pure new horror success is The Exit 8 — a single-verb OBSERVATION game with no chase, no combat, and 127M visits in three months. The throne room is full of games that aren't actually about fear; nobody on the platform currently owns modern single-player psychological dread.
