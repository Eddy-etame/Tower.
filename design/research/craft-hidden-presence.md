# The Hidden Presence archetype (doubt -> dread -> revelation) — craft study: Amnesia's sanity/gaze pressure, Phasmophobia's evidence loop, sound-shadow/absence techniques, Silent Hill fog, recontextualizing reveals (P.T., Anatomy), false-presence fairness (Phasmophobia's Mimic, Darkwood), sustained-ambiguity pacing (Alien: Isolation's director, Duskers)

> T5 research dossier - craft:HiddenPresence - generated 2026-07-06. Every finding: mechanism -> causal chain -> verdict -> Threshold translation -> where it lands -> source.

## Summary

The Hidden Presence lives or dies on one axis: can the game keep "maybe there's nothing" alive without the player either disengaging (boredom) or feeling cheated (randomness). The masters solve disengagement by giving the player falsifiable WORK — Phasmophobia's instrument loop and Duskers' sensor feeds turn waiting into active hypothesis-testing, while Alien: Isolation's two-brain director paces evidence on a tension budget instead of timers. They solve inevitability by making the presence physically real and honest from frame one: P.T.'s Lisa is literally attached to the player's back the whole game, so every rear-anchored sound was a true emission and the reveal recontextualizes hours of play as evidence in hindsight. Fairness comes from giving even the lies a consistent fingerprint — Phasmophobia's Mimic always fakes the SAME evidence category, so deception itself becomes learnable. The single biggest lesson for Project 001: simulate the presence for real (server-side, honest-in-geometry) from Act I and hide it only from the senses, never from the simulation — ambiguity should be a property of the player's perception, not of the world's truth.

## Findings

### 1. [ADAPT] Hidden Presence Acts I-II (doubt phase); also the Silent Witness's being-studied pressure; system: any presence/attention stat

- **Mechanism:** Amnesia's sanity meter hides all numbers and never states consequences: an inventory-only 'brain rotting' visual plus vague text ('a slight headache'). Thomas Grip: players 'make up their own theories of how it worked, and the lack of information made them more frightened about it.' The informational gap is the mechanic.
- **Causal chain:** unquantified consequence (no number, no stated rule) -> player invents private worst-case theories and self-imposes avoidance rituals -> dread manufactured by the player's own imagination, at zero content cost
- **Threshold translation:** Any Hidden Presence 'attention' state is tracked server-side but NEVER surfaced as a meter, number, or tip. Symptoms only: breath sound changes, hand tremor on lantern, text-free bodily cues. Critically, unlike Amnesia we never make a false explicit claim — ambiguity is allowed only where no testable rule can form; every rule the player CAN test must be true (Constitution: trust inviolable).
- **Source:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-i-amnesia-i-s-sanity-meter-

### 2. [ADAPT] Hidden Presence Act II-III (dread escalation); system: entity visibility/withdrawal logic

- **Mechanism:** Amnesia's fear-of-looking: looking directly at a monster drains sanity and blurs/shakes the screen until it is unreadable, forcing 'a frantic, sideways glance style of play that mimics real-world panic.' The player voluntarily destroys their own information channel.
- **Causal chain:** cost attached to direct observation -> player averts gaze, navigates by periphery and audio -> the unseen entity is rendered by imagination, which outperforms any mesh -> dread that survives repeat encounters
- **Threshold translation:** Invert it honestly: the Threshold presence WITHDRAWS under direct scrutiny (stared-at evidence stops occurring; the corner you watch stays still, the one you left changes — Awareness technique #4). Looking is never punished by a hidden stat; it is answered by absence. The player learns 'I only perceive it when I'm not trying to' — which is a true, testable rule, and it weaponizes peripheral attention on a small phone screen.
- **Source:** https://www.everythingisscary.com/play/2015/6/11/breathing-new-life-into-death-amnesia-the-dark-descent + https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-i-amnesia-i-s-sanity-meter-

### 3. [REFUSE] All archetypes; system: onboarding/tips (we ship none that state mechanics), death/info screens

- **Mechanism:** Amnesia's actual lie: an early tip claims looking at monsters helps them find you, but per Grip 'there is no fail state' — sanity has 'no real repercussion on gameplay, including whether the monster will be able to locate them,' and monsters silently despawn. The fear is placebo.
- **Causal chain:** explicit false rule statement -> player obeys a phantom rule and is terrified on first play -> on discovery (wiki, replay, YouTube), all fear collapses retroactively and the game 'loses' its scare permanently — a refund on trust
- **Threshold translation:** We refuse stated lies outright. On Roblox the discovery half-life is days, not years — our 9-13yo audience lives in wikis, Discord and TikTok tell-alls, and Hidden Presence is precisely the archetype players will datamine. Our version of the placebo: say NOTHING mechanical, ever. The world implies; the player theorizes; every theory they can verify verifies true. Ambiguity through silence is renewable; ambiguity through lies is single-use.
- **Source:** https://screenrant.com/amnesia-dark-descent-sanity-meter-fake-monster/

### 4. [ADAPT] Hidden Presence Acts I-III; core question 'is there even something here?'; system: diegetic detection item; 2-3 approaches requirement (instrument route vs pure-observation route)

- **Mechanism:** Phasmophobia's evidence loop: senses suspect, instruments confirm. EMF, UV, spirit box each have a hard threshold that converts suspicion into recorded fact, each confirmation narrows the ghost-type hypothesis space, and gathering requires physically approaching the danger — some ghosts (Raiju) are even enraged by the instruments themselves.
- **Causal chain:** unverifiable suspicion + a falsifiable test the player can run -> player walks TOWARD the danger to run the test, stays engaged during 'nothing happens' stretches because waiting is measuring -> tension sustained by the push-pull of needing proximity for proof
- **Threshold translation:** Give the Hidden Presence encounter one diegetic 'instrument' found in-world (a maintenance CO2 gauge, a dead-reckoning compass that drifts near it, a tape recorder that captures what ears missed). The instrument confirms but never locates — it converts 'is there something here?' into 'WHERE and WHAT is here?' one notch at a time, and using it is itself exposure. This is our disengagement solve: doubt phases must contain a verification VERB, not just atmosphere.
- **Source:** https://www.gamespot.com/articles/for-phasmophobia-to-be-so-terrifying-the-devil-is-in-the-details/1100-6527456/ + https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Evidence

### 5. [ADOPT] Hidden Presence Act II (planted doubt) and the false-presence design of all four archetypes; system: fake-signal authoring rules

- **Mechanism:** Phasmophobia's Mimic: the one ghost that fabricates evidence always fakes the SAME category (Ghost Orbs), creating an evidence-count paradox — four confirmations on a three-evidence difficulty. The deception has a consistent fingerprint, so veterans identify the liar BY its lie.
- **Causal chain:** a false positive with a fixed, learnable signature -> first encounter: misdirection and doubt about the whole toolkit; later encounters: players audit evidence counts and catch the lie -> paranoia matures into earned mastery instead of resentment
- **Threshold translation:** This is the fair-tell law for false presence, confirmed in the wild: when the Threshold plants fake signals (horror-craft arsenal: fake audio events, fake sigils), the fake must carry ONE invariant property — wrong reverb tail, direction offset, a repetition too exact. First contact deceives; observation converts deception into information. Community debate ('why would devs mislead on purpose?') shows the tolerance line: misleading is loved when detectable, hated when arbitrary.
- **Source:** https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mimic + https://steamcommunity.com/app/739630/discussions/0/510696183800292629/

### 6. [ADOPT] Hidden Presence Acts I-II primarily; reusable for Violent Rhythm pre-hunt telegraph; system: ambience layer manager + presence proximity ducking

- **Mechanism:** Sound-shadow / absence-as-information: layered ambience where a presence is known by what STOPS — songbird layers removed near danger, 'the absence of expected noise amplifies fear,' ambience cut seconds before a beat. State of the art is layer-ducking tied to threat proximity so the quiet zone is literally centered on the presence.
- **Causal chain:** an expected, previously-established sound disappears -> player freezes, scans, holds still to listen (silence makes them produce the silence too) -> the mind 'instinctively tries to fill the vacuum with worst-case scenarios' -> self-generated dread with zero assets spent
- **Threshold translation:** Our Absence (#2) executed as honest geometry: the Threshold hum (vents, pipes, distant machinery — no music, per directive) is a persistent bed the player is taught to stop hearing in Act I; the presence carries a server-side 'silence radius' that ducks bed layers by proximity. The quiet is a TRUE positional signal — walk the gradient of missing sound and you are triangulating the entity with your ears. Absence becomes our sonar. Requires Act I to establish the baseline before Act II can subtract it.
- **Source:** https://www.wayline.io/blog/silence-is-scary-sound-design-horror-games + https://gamesounddesign.com/Silence-In-Sound-Design.html + https://medium.com/@GameAudio/the-art-of-fear-the-psychology-of-sound-design-in-horror-games-d85b9854c3b0

### 7. [ADOPT] Hidden Presence Act IV (climax/revelation) — candidate one-sentence climax: 'the player plays back their own footsteps and hears a second set'; system: entity lifecycle (spawned Act I, revealed Act IV)

- **Mechanism:** P.T.'s Lisa is attached to the player's back the moment you pick up the flashlight and stays there the entire game — Lance McDonald's camera hack proved every rear-anchored breath, shadow flicker and turn-around-to-nothing was a TRUE emission from a physically present, simulated entity the camera simply never framed.
- **Causal chain:** honest cues from a real but never-framed source -> player repeatedly turns around and finds nothing, begins doubting their own senses -> at reveal, every prior 'false alarm' retroactively becomes evidence -> the inevitable-in-hindsight shockwave: 'it was here the WHOLE time', and dread persists after the game closes
- **Threshold translation:** THE blueprint for our revelation act: instantiate the presence server-side in Act I as a real object with real position, emitting honest positional audio and honest environmental side-effects — hidden only by framing, light, and the player's assumptions. Revelation = the moment the player acquires the one viewpoint (a reflection, a recording played back, a camera feed) that frames what was always simulated. It feels inevitable because it IS: no retcon, no spawn-in. Understanding makes it worse: they replay the whole encounter in memory and find it everywhere.
- **Source:** https://www.gamesradar.com/a-pt-camera-hack-reveals-lisa-is-always-behind-you-and-its-absolutely-terrifying/ + https://geekculture.co/camera-hack-reveals-lisa-always-behind-you-in-hideo-kojima-p-t-and-its-terrifying/

### 8. [ADAPT] Hidden Presence Act IV alternative climax + The Threshold's world identity; also feeds Silent Witness (the watcher with no body)

- **Mechanism:** Anatomy (Kitty Horrorshow): the reveal recontextualizes the container itself — cassette tapes teach 'a house is like a body' until the player realizes the house IS the presence; rooms are 'gently impossible' in size, and the game degrades across mandatory replays so the space itself turns hostile.
- **Causal chain:** violation of the domestic-safety schema without any visible entity -> player searches for a monster that is the room they are standing in -> revelation inverts the search: there was never something IN the house to find -> understanding multiplies terror because there is nowhere the presence is not
- **Threshold translation:** The purest fit with The Threshold's soul: impossible + indifferent = the strongest Hidden Presence answer to 'is there even something here?' may be 'there is no THING — the place itself is attending to you.' Adapt the gentle-impossibility craft (rooms subtly wrong in size, connections that quietly disagree) as Act I-II evidence, and make the Act IV revelation an inversion of the question rather than a creature reveal. Refuse the meta/file-degradation layer — wrong platform, wrong audience, and it flirts with lying about the client.
- **Source:** https://www.gamerswithglasses.com/features/whats-in-a-house + https://medium.com/@abrightfar/review-of-kitty-horrorshow-s-anatomy-729f68ef9c88 + https://gamestudies.org/2403/articles/leblanc

### 9. [ADAPT] Hidden Presence Act I-III spatial pressure; system: per-room atmosphere/density; platform: low-end mobile rendering

- **Mechanism:** Silent Hill's fog: a PS1 draw-distance workaround inverted into the series' defining presence — a constant-radius occlusion that makes every step a reveal event and reads as the town itself withholding information; 'the world itself felt alive with menace' without spawning anything.
- **Causal chain:** vision truncated at a constant, fair radius -> every forward step becomes a small wager; player slows, listens, and treats empty streets as loaded -> sustained low-grade dread across hours, with the occluder itself becoming the perceived entity
- **Threshold translation:** Fog is presence-shaped occlusion AND our mobile ally: it raises scene luminance (readable on bright phone screens where raw darkness dies), hides draw distance on low-end devices exactly as it did on PS1, and its density can breathe per room as the Threshold's 'attention.' Rule: fog radius changes are slow and rule-bound (a room trait, learnable), never twitched to spring surprises — occlusion must stay fair. Darkness-as-occlusion fails on mobile; fog-as-occlusion is the same dread that ships on a phone.
- **Source:** https://retro-replay.com/retroreplayfyi-the-accidental-horror-of-silent-hills-fog/ + https://screenrant.com/silent-hill-2-remake-fog-darkness-good/

### 10. [ADOPT] Hidden Presence Acts II-III and the archetype's whole real-vs-imagined tell design; system: two-pool audio taxonomy (threat/ambient)

- **Mechanism:** Darkwood's night audio grammar: each sound class maps to a threat state (creaking wood = something testing the barricade, shuffling = it is inside), while OTHER sounds are pure ambient red herrings — 'weird knocking might signal something stalking your compound or might just be ambient' — and enemies genuinely obey hearing/vision rules (stand still in darkness and a Biter passes 2 meters away).
- **Causal chain:** honest threat-sounds interleaved with harmless ambient sounds sharing the same soundscape -> player must CLASSIFY every noise against a learnable grammar instead of merely reacting -> paranoia with homework: doubt is the gameplay, and surviving a night by ear feels earned
- **Threshold translation:** This is the fair-tell architecture for real-vs-imagined signals, proven over a whole game: build a two-pool sound system — threat pool (each sound a truthful state broadcast: position, intent, phase) and ambient pool (the Threshold settling: pipe knocks, structural groans), separated by consistent learnable properties (threat sounds are positional, repeat with intent, and correlate with physical consequence; ambient sounds are diffuse and consequence-free). The player's skill curve IS learning the grammar — doubt is front-loaded, mastery is real, and the world never lied.
- **Source:** https://gamepathguides.com/articles/darkwood-hidden-mechanics.html + https://gamecritics.com/daniel-weissenberger/darkwood-review/

### 11. [ADAPT] Hidden Presence pacing across all five acts; system: encounter director / tension budget service (server-side)

- **Mechanism:** Alien: Isolation's two-brain system: an omniscient Director that 'always knows where you are' but only manages a menace gauge (tension budget), feeding vague search-area hints to a genuinely ignorant Xenomorph agent that must perceive the player itself. If the player is safe too long, the Director nudges the alien nearer; if pressured too long, it pulls it away.
- **Causal chain:** systemically paced closeness with agent-level ignorance -> player can never pattern a timer ('it's been quiet too long' is felt, never scheduled) -> ambiguity stays alive for hours without boredom spikes or scripted unfairness
- **Threshold translation:** For Hidden Presence, run a doubt director on a dread budget: it schedules AMBIGUITY BEATS (a sound-shadow, a moved object, an instrument blip) when engagement decays, and schedules NOTHING when the player is already saturated — but the presence-agent itself only acts on its own honest senses, so no beat ever violates geometry or rules. Also honor its craft rule of drip-fed behaviors: reveal one new evidence type per act, never the whole vocabulary at once. Caution from its reception (Game Studies): the instant players PERCEIVE directorial puppeteering, fairness collapses — the director may only spend beats the simulation can truthfully deliver.
- **Source:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-perfect-organism-the-ai-of-alien-isolation + https://gamestudies.org/2002/articles/jaroslav_svelch + https://medium.com/@aetosdios27/the-illusion-of-intelligence-a-technical-breakdown-of-alien-isolations-ai-b2d7c9927d02

### 12. [ADAPT] Hidden Presence Act II-III verification beats; system: diegetic camera/sensor props; budget: art-light dread

- **Mechanism:** Duskers' sensor-only perception: the entire game is seen through degradable drone feeds and a motion scanner that reports 'something moved' — never what, never exactly where; feeds corrupt under radiation, and rooms are void until physically entered. The interface's unreliability IS the horror.
- **Causal chain:** all information mediated by a lossy, abstract instrument -> the player's imagination renders every blip into a monster worse than any model; verifying costs a resource that can be destroyed -> dread from pure abstraction, plus genuine dilemma (spend a drone to know, or act on doubt)
- **Threshold translation:** Validates that Hidden Presence needs almost no monster art budget: a motion blip, a corrupted frame, a temperature number moving the wrong way outperforms a rendered creature — enormous for Roblox low-end rendering. Adapt as moments, not the whole frame: a wall-mounted facility monitor showing a corridor feed where SOMETHING crosses; a sensor the player must leave behind (and risk) to verify a room. Refuse the full mediated-interface structure — command lines and multi-window feeds die on a phone screen and on our young audience.
- **Source:** https://www.popmatters.com/duskers-2495431388.html + https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/duskers-review/1900-6416465/

### 13. [ADOPT] Hidden Presence five-act pacing (Discovery/Escalation boundaries); the encounter director's beat economy

- **Mechanism:** Tension-curve law (Bycer): 'there must always be a build up followed by a release, you can't just do one or the other for horror to work.' Dead Space fails by constant assault (desensitization); Amnesia: A Machine for Pigs fails by endless dread with no cathartic confirmation (disengagement). Confirmed presence beats also decay on reuse ('the first time that dog jumped through the window... repeated plays not so much').
- **Causal chain:** unbroken ambiguity with zero confirmation -> player's hypothesis 'there is nothing here' hardens through repeated non-events -> attention withdraws and dread becomes boredom; conversely each confirmation spends the unknown and must escalate or recontextualize to stay potent
- **Threshold translation:** The doubt phase must be PUNCTUATED: every N minutes of 'maybe nothing', the world pays out one small, undeniable-but-unexplained confirmation (the door you closed is open; one instrument reading that cannot be ambient) — enough to re-arm doubt without answering the core question. Budget confirmations like currency: each one converts a little uncertainty into a little certainty, so the five-act arc must spend them in escalating denominations, with the P.T.-style recontextualization as the final, biggest note. Short Roblox sessions compress N aggressively.
- **Source:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-balancing-act-of-tension-in-horror-game-design

## Trust breaks (their haters are our free QA)

- Amnesia: The Dark Descent — the game explicitly tells players looking at monsters helps them find you, but sanity has no such effect and monsters silently despawn; when players discover this (wikis, replays), the fear collapses retroactively and replay dread dies to zero. A stated lie is single-use horror. (screenrant.com/amnesia-dark-descent-sanity-meter-fake-monster)
- Phasmophobia — community debate over the Mimic's fake Ghost Orbs ('Why would the developers mislead the players on purpose?') marks the exact tolerance line: deception with a learnable fingerprint is celebrated, deception perceived as arbitrary is called unfair; on reduced-evidence difficulties the loop degrades into guessing, which players report as RNG, not investigation. (steamcommunity.com/app/739630 discussions)
- Roblox DOORS / Screech — players report pre-emptively LOWERING their volume because Screech's punishment bite is loud, i.e., the punishment for an audio-dependent mechanic trains players to degrade the very channel the mechanic needs; muted/low-volume mobile players get hit by what is functionally an untelegraphed attack. Audio-only tells without a visual shadow are a trust break on our platform. (doors-game.fandom.com Screech discussions; playbite.com guides)
- Alien: Isolation — reception research shows the moment players sense the Director puppeteering the alien (uncanny backtracking, 'it always knows'), the fear reframes as cheating; systemic pacing must never be perceivable as authorial intervention. (gamestudies.org/2002/articles/jaroslav_svelch)
- Amnesia: A Machine for Pigs — endless dread with no confirmation or release produced disengagement, not terror; 'maybe nothing' sustained too long without payout is experienced as the game wasting the player's time. (gamedeveloper.com balancing-act-of-tension article)
- Roblox platform-wide — DevForum threads document the same lighting settings rendering significantly DARKER on mobile than on PC; horror builders tuning darkness on desktop monitors ship rooms mobile players literally cannot read, and those players don't complain, they leave. Free QA: test every subtle visual cue on a real phone in a lit room. (devforum.roblox.com/t/game-is-darker-on-mobile/2806476)

## Mobile notes

Phone speakers roll off from about 1kHz and produce almost nothing below roughly 220-400Hz (Audiokinetic measurements; sensitive bands cluster at 220, 500, 1k, 2.5k, 3.5k, 10-14kHz, usually mono) — so the classic hidden-presence toolkit of sub-bass rumble and LFE 'presence weight' simply does not exist for ~70% of our players; presence audio cues must live in the 500Hz-5kHz mid band (breath, tapping, paper-dry textures) rather than low drones. The single cue that survives EVERY speaker, every volume level, and even partial muting is ABSENCE — a bed of ambience that stops reproduces perfectly on any hardware, which is a physics-level argument for sound-shadow as our #1 hidden-presence channel on mobile. Because many mobile players play quiet or muted, every audio tell needs a visual shadow (dust settling, a light losing confidence, geometry that changed) — DOORS' Screech shows what happens otherwise: players get punished through a channel they turned off, and they learn to distrust the game rather than the entity. Visually, Roblox renders darker on mobile than PC (documented DevForum issue) and small bright screens in lit rooms crush shadow detail, so subtle cues must be carried by luminance-separated silhouettes, rim light, and fog (which RAISES scene brightness while occluding — Silent Hill's trick is mobile-native) rather than by darkness; every 'did I see that?' cue gets an on-device test in a lit room, per RULES.md #8. Short mobile sessions mean the full doubt->dread->revelation arc must resolve within one sitting (10-15 min), so the confirmation-beat economy compresses: fewer, larger denominations, and the recontextualizing reveal can never be parked behind a second session.

## The surprise

P.T.'s Lisa — the most famous 'is something here?' ambiguity in horror history — was implemented as a fully HONEST mechanic: she is physically attached to the player's back from the flashlight pickup onward, so every rear-anchored breath and empty turn-around was a true emission from a real simulated object, hidden only by camera framing. We expected the state of the art to be smoke and mirrors (scripted audio, spawn-on-reveal); instead the deepest hidden presence ever shipped is honest-in-geometry, which independently validates Runtime Studio's trust law: you do not need to lie to the player to make them doubt their senses — you need to simulate the truth and control only what frames it. Second-order surprise: Amnesia, the counterexample built on an actual lie, is exactly the game whose fear famously dies on replay once the lie is public — the two flagship cases land on opposite sides of our Constitution and the honest one aged better.
